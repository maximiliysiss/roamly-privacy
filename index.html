Privacy Policy — Roamly

Last updated: May 2026

Data Storage

Roamly stores all data locally on your device. No personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared with third parties.

Location Data

Location is used solely to track your walking routes and reveal the map. Location data never leaves your device.

Third-party Services

Roamly uses OpenStreetMap tiles for map display (openstreetmap.org/copyright) and Overpass API for points of interest. No personal data is sent to these services.
