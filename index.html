Privacy Policy — Roamly

Last updated: May 2026

Data Storage

Roamly stores all data locally on your device. No personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared with third parties. We operate no servers and require no account or sign-up.

Location Data

Location is used solely to track your walking routes and reveal your personal exploration map. The app accesses location in the background so the map can update while your phone is in your pocket. Location data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

Data We Collect

Roamly stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • GPS coordinates of your walking routes
  • Explored map tiles
  • Achievement progress and unlock dates
  • Cached points of interest from OpenStreetMap
  • App preferences and settings

None of this data is linked to your identity or shared with anyone.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored on your device for as long as the app is installed. No data is sent to or retained on any external server.

How to delete your data: You can permanently delete all app data at any time by going to your phone's Settings → Apps → Roamly → Storage → Clear Data. Uninstalling the app also removes all associated data from your device. Since Roamly has no backend or user accounts, no additional deletion request is necessary.

Third-party Services

Roamly uses the following third-party services solely to display map content and points of interest:

  • OpenStreetMap — map tile display ( openstreetmap.org/copyright)
  • Overpass API — public points of interest data
  • Wikimedia API — place descriptions and photos where available

These services receive only the map coordinates needed to load content for your current area. No personal data or identifiers are transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Roamly does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. The app collects no personal information from any user regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
